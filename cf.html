Cut Fruit & Mealtime Approvals

1 · Pack & size changes

AFI changed the pack or size on an ingredient we use. Pick the one that's right for the recipe math. Press Remember so it isn't asked again while AFI keeps sending the same thing.

2 · Items gone from AFI

These ingredient numbers aren't in this week's AFI files. Suggestions come from every AFI feed (produce, meat, dairy, supplies…), including items numbered next to it in the same product line. Pick one, type a number, or skip: this week only, or until AFI carries it again. Items on a skipped ingredient keep last week's cost.

3 · Cheaper produce options

Same product, 5% or more cheaper per pound (or per bin for count-sized fruit). A swap to a different size rescales the recipe yield and flags the item for a test cut.

4 · Retails outside the margin band

Only items outside the band are listed: cut fruit 45–60%, Mealtime 40–50%. Each item gets up to two prices just under the band (marked in red) and up to four inside it, all ending in 9; the small figure is the margin each one gives. Type any other price and its margin shows next to the box. Food King retail is Lowe's ÷ 1.1 and isn't asked.
